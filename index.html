Warranty and return policy

Every product we sell is original and comes with warranty. Keep your invoice and the box until the warranty period ends.

Mobiles1 year official warranty
Cameras1 to 2 years, see product page
TVs1 to 2 years, panel warranty included
Accessories6 months to lifetime, see product page

What is covered

  • Manufacturing defects in hardware
  • Software and firmware faults
  • Battery and charger faults within warranty
  • Display or panel defects not caused by damage

What is not covered

  • Physical damage, cracked screens and bent frames
  • Water or liquid damage (unless the product is rated waterproof)
  • Burns from wrong voltage or power surge
  • Opened, repaired or modified by an unauthorized person
  • Missing or tampered serial number or IMEI

Replacement policy

  • If a product is dead on arrival, report within 7 days for replacement
  • Product must be in original box with all accessories and invoice
  • Replacement is subject to stock and manufacturer inspection

How to claim warranty

  1. Message us on WhatsApp with your invoice and a short video of the issue.
  2. Bring the product to our shop at ECB Chattar, Cantonment, Dhaka.
  3. We inspect it and send it to the service center if needed.
  4. We update you until it is repaired or replaced.

Warranty terms follow the brand's official policy. Our policy may be updated at any time.
